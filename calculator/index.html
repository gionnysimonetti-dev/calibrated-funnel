The 5x rule of thumb

  • Step under 5xThe third stage is rules, not a model. A middle model costs more than it saves.
  • 5x or more, weak signalA middle model pays for itself. Rules still help if they cover enough queries.
  • 5x or more, clear signalTwo models are enough.

What this is

A simulation on 100,000 synthetic queries, run in your browser. Difficulty, model ability and the confidence signal follow simple Gaussian assumptions. The threshold is chosen on one sample and the result is read on another.

It tells you which designs are worth measuring. It is not a forecast for a real system.

Four numbers to measure first

  1. Run the smallest model on a sample with known answers: the share it gets right with high confidence.
  2. The share of queries a deterministic step can answer outright.
  3. Compute per query for each model you can run.
  4. The AUROC of the small model's confidence against right and wrong answers.

What the saving does not cover

Compute, energy and latency go down. Memory does not: every model in the cascade stays loaded. The rule stage is idealised: 99.5% accurate on what it answers, at 1% of the small model's cost.
